# Inter-rater agreement summary (29 included studies)

## Figure 5: validation depth and deployment readiness

- Validation depth: quadratic-weighted kappa = 0.98 (95% CI 0.90-1.00); exact agreement 28/29.
- Deployment readiness: quadratic-weighted kappa = 0.87 (95% CI 0.64-0.98); exact agreement 25/29.
- Pooled across both axes: quadratic-weighted kappa = 0.93 (95% CI 0.82-0.99).
- Differences were resolved by consensus against the full texts.

## Figure 6: nine reporting and validation items

- Quadratic-weighted kappa = 0.97 (95% CI 0.94-0.99); exact agreement 253/260 applicable cells.
- The seven item-level differences were each one level apart and were resolved by consensus against the full texts.
- The agreed 29-study matrix is available in `Figure6_final_consensus.xlsx` and in the consolidated workbook.

## Sensitivity analysis

| Measure | Unweighted | Linear | Quadratic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Figure 5 validation | 0.95 (0.83-1.00) | 0.96 (0.86-1.00) | 0.98 (0.90-1.00) |
| Figure 5 readiness | 0.80 (0.59-0.95) | 0.83 (0.63-0.97) | 0.87 (0.64-0.98) |
| Figure 5 pooled | 0.88 (0.77-0.97) | 0.90 (0.79-0.97) | 0.93 (0.82-0.99) |
| Figure 6 nine items | 0.95 (0.90-0.99) | 0.96 (0.92-0.99) | 0.97 (0.94-0.99) |

Agreement is robust to the weighting scheme. Quadratic weights are reported in
the manuscript because both scoring frameworks are ordinal. NA cells are
excluded pairwise. Confidence intervals use a cluster bootstrap over the 29
studies (10,000 resamples; fixed random seed 2026).
